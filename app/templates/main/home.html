About AgriVision Nepal

AgriVision Nepal is an agricultural intelligence platform crafted specifically for farmers, growers, agronomists, and home gardeners across Nepal. By connecting plant visual recognition with Kalimati wholesale market data and Nepal-specific agronomic research, AgriVision bridges the gap between field observation and market economics.

Whether you are cultivating crops in the fertile Terai plains, mid-hill terraces, or river valleys, AgriVision helps you verify crop varieties, plan planting calendars, apply balanced fertilizers, protect against climate risks, and determine the most profitable seasons to harvest and sell.

How to Use AgriVision (4 Easy Steps)

01
1. Capture or Upload a Photo Tap 'Open Camera' to take a live photo in your field or garden, or use 'Upload Photo' to select an image from your phone or computer. Ensure leaves, flowers, or fruit are clearly visible in natural daylight.
02
2. Instant Crop Identification Instantly discover the crop's local Nepali name (e.g. गोलभेंडा, फर्सी, काउली), standard English common name, scientific Latin classification, and botanical family.
03
3. Agronomy, Fertilizer & Climate Guide Check when to plant in Nepal (Falgun–Chaitra vs. Jestha–Ashadh), days to maturity, favorable temperatures, risk warnings (frost, waterlogging), and a step-by-step fertilizer plan (FYM, DAP, Potash, Urea).
04
4. Market Rates & Selling Strategy Review today's wholesale benchmark price from Kalimati Wholesale Market, Kathmandu. Discover peak profit months, off-season tunnel advantages, and storage advice to sell at highest rates.

Tips for Accurate Results

— Take photos in bright, natural daylight rather than in dim indoor light.
— Get close to distinctive plant parts like leaves, flowers, or fruit rather than a distant panorama.
— Switch anytime between English and Nepali using the toggle button in the top banner or navbar.
— Revisit your previous scans and price history anytime from the 'Records' page.
